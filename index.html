Tiny Thief VPK Builder

The Tiny Thief 1.2.1 XAPK for Android, made into a PS Vita VPK on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

1. Your Tiny Thief XAPK XAPK ONLY

This builder takes one file: the Tiny Thief XAPK (a .xapk file), which holds the APK and the game's data file (the OBB) together. An APK will not work, nor an OBB on its own.

Drop your Tiny Thief XAPK here or click to choose the .xapk file

    Made from the pictures inside the APK, shown as they will look on the Vita. Click a picture (or drop an image on it) to use your own instead: it is scaled to fill the space and cropped from the centre.
